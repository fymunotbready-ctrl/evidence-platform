# Access Control Policy
All employees must use multi-factor authentication (MFA) for email, source code, and cloud consoles.
We use single sign-on (SSO) through Okta for all business applications.
Access is granted on a least-privilege basis and approved by the system owner.
User access reviews are performed every quarter.
Accounts of departing employees are disabled within 24 hours of termination.
Shared accounts are prohibited.
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long.
Production database access is limited to the on-call engineering team.
