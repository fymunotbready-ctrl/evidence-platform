# Timely Filing

Claims must be submitted within 90 days of the date of service. Exceptions to timely filing are considered when the provider supplies proof of timely submission, such as a clearinghouse acceptance report, or documentation of payer error.

# Incomplete Claims

Claims missing required information such as a diagnosis code or modifier are denied as incomplete. A corrected claim may be submitted within 365 days of the date of service.

# Duplicate Claims

A claim identical to a previously processed claim is denied as a duplicate. Verify the status of the original claim before taking further action.
