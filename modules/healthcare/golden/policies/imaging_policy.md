# Advanced Imaging

MRI of the lumbar spine is considered medically necessary when the member has completed at least 6 weeks of conservative therapy without improvement, or presents with red-flag symptoms such as progressive neurological deficit or suspected cauda equina syndrome.

CT of the head is considered medically necessary following acute head trauma with loss of consciousness or new neurological symptoms.

# Prior Authorization

Advanced imaging (MRI, CT, PET) requires prior authorization. Retroactive authorization may be requested within 14 days of the date of service when the service was urgent or emergent and prior authorization could not reasonably be obtained.
