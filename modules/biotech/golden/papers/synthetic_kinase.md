# Abstract

KRX1 is a fictional serine kinase overexpressed in synthetic tumor cell lines. Treatment with the inhibitor compound SX-12 (CHEMBL999901) reduced xenograft tumor volume by 42% compared with vehicle after 21 days. SX-12 did not change body weight in treated mice.

# Methods

Mice were randomized into two groups of 10 animals. SX-12 was administered by oral gavage once daily for 21 days. Tumor volume was measured every three days using calipers.

# Results

KRX1 expression was 3.5-fold higher in tumor cell lines than in normal cell lines. SX-12 inhibited KRX1 kinase activity with an IC50 of 12 nM in biochemical assays. Phosphorylation of the substrate protein SUB2 decreased after SX-12 treatment.

# Limitations

The study used a single cell line and a small sample size. Long-term toxicity was not evaluated.
