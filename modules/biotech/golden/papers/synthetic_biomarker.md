# Abstract

BMK7 levels were measured in 120 synthetic plasma samples. BMK7 was higher in the disease group than in controls (median 8.2 versus 3.1 ng/mL). The assay showed an inter-day coefficient of variation below 8%.

# Methods

Samples were stored at minus 80 degrees Celsius until analysis. BMK7 was quantified by sandwich ELISA.

# Results

Receiver operating characteristic analysis gave an area under the curve of 0.81. Age and sex did not affect BMK7 levels.

# Limitations

The cohort came from a single center and was not validated externally.
