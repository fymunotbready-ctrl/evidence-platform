# Abstract

We determined a synthetic structure of the fictional protein ZED2 (UniProt: Q0ABC1) at 2.1 angstrom resolution. ZED2 forms a homodimer through a conserved coiled-coil interface. A loop between residues 112 and 130 closes over the active site when substrate is bound.

# Methods

Crystals were grown by vapor diffusion at 18 degrees Celsius. Diffraction data were collected at a synthetic synchrotron beamline.

# Results

Mutation of residue Arg118 to alanine abolished substrate binding. The dimer interface buries 1,450 square angstroms of surface area. ZED2 is expressed mainly in liver tissue in the synthetic dataset.

# Limitations

Only the apo and substrate-bound forms were solved; no inhibitor-bound structure is available.
